# Data

The local analysis expects `QQQ_clean_5min.parquet` and `SPY_clean_5min.parquet` in this folder. These cleaned five-minute files are excluded from Git, so they are not available to someone who only clones the repository. The current files cover 7 March 2022 to 16 October 2026; no later dates are currently available locally. The source is IEX-based market data, not consolidated volume across all US exchanges. Missing scheduled bars are retained as missing in analysis and are not interpolated. Keep the original source files unchanged and check the data provider's licence before sharing any data.
